  the hot bound and 50 ms BELOW the cold bound.

  VERDICT: NOT an eviction. The hot threshold sits inside the natural jitter
  band, so ordinary variance trips it. Genuinely evicted data would read
  ~50 ms, two orders of magnitude higher.
  FIX NEEDED: calibrate hot_threshold from the observed distribution
  (e.g. mean + 3 sigma of a warm-up run) rather than a fixed value, or the
  script will keep reporting phantom evictions.
